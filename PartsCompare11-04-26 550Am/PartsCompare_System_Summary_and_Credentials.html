📋 Module 5: BOM Preparer Workspace & Multi-Objective Sourcing (#/lists)

5A. Dynamic Sourcing Strategies & MCDA Optimization

Purpose: Evaluates bill of materials component lines against accredited supplier quotations using four algorithmic procurement strategies:

Sourcing Strategy Algorithmic Principle Optimal HVAC Scenario
1. Lowest Cost Greedy unit price minimization per line item. Standard routine repair where minimizing direct material budget is paramount.
2. Supplier Consolidation Maximizes total line items fulfilled by a single accredited supplier to minimize logistics/freight costs. Urgent turnaround overhaul where picking up all parts from one vendor saves critical technician travel time.
3. Resilience (Stock Buffer) Selects candidates with the highest recorded warehouse stock headroom over project requirements. High-volume commercial projects where inventory shortfalls cause expensive jobsite delays.
4. Balanced (Dynamic MCDA) Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis weighting Cost (40%), Stock (30%), Freshness (20%), and Consolidation (10%). Enterprise procurement balancing price competitiveness, vendor reliability, and data recency.
💡 Real-World Scenario: For Job #AC-2026-088 (Split-Type AC Overhaul), Dave switches from Lowest Cost (split between 2 vendors) to Supplier Consolidation. C&C fulfills all required parts, reducing delivery fees and technician pickup delays.

🔒 Module 6: 14-Day Freshness Rule & Preserved Sourcing Summaries (#/summaries)

6A. Governance Rule: Offer Freshness Policy (14 Calendar Days)

Supply chain prices in the Philippines fluctuate rapidly. PartsCompare strictly flags any quote verified more than 14 days ago as Excluded: Stale Quote. Stale quotes cannot be signed off until re-verified.

6B. Governance Rule: "Unresolved is Never ₱0.00"

In incomplete BOMs, assuming an unquoted part costs zero leads to dangerous budget underestimation. PartsCompare excludes unresolved lines from the subtotal and flags the job sheet until an accredited quote is verified.

6C. Preserved Sourcing Summaries & Tamper-Proof Snapshots

Clicking "Freeze Sourcing Summary" cryptographically locks the agreed prices, chosen vendors, and rule settings. Future catalog edits will never alter historical sign-offs, guaranteeing 100% audit integrity.

💡 Real-World Scenario: Six months after completing Job #AC-2026-088, the building owner audits the billing. Karen opens the frozen summary; the exact ₱3,795.00 breakdown signed off by Dave on 11/4/2026 remains preserved verbatim.
